# Code of Conduct

Glowberry is a personal artwork developed in the open. Contributors and discussants are expected to:

- Be respectful in issues, PRs, Discussions, and related spaces.
- Stay within the artistic and process boundaries in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [docs/BRIEF.md](docs/BRIEF.md).
- Not harass, demean, or dox anyone.
- Not invent permissions, quotes, site agreements, or funding commitments.

Report concerns to **hello@adamsimms.xyz**. The artist may refuse, edit, or remove contributions that violate these norms or the piece’s ethics.
