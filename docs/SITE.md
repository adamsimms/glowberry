# Glowberry: NL site longlist

Living longlist for a reachable Newfoundland outdoor site. **No parcel is chosen. No permissions are claimed.** Moodboard photos stay local (not in this repo).

Locked landscape character (`docs/BRIEF.md` §2): inland of shore / rock or cliff plateau, open coastal view. Dusk / night is the show.

## Method

Fogo Island and the Bonavista Peninsula are **regions**, not parcels. They are too large to pick a tickle from a map in Montreal. The working method is: use an existing introduction, then ask that partner for a **recommendation or collaboration** on where a ~6 ft walk-around fruit could live (wind, land, neighbours, stewardship). That is not a permission. It is how a parcel gets named.

Avalon named options (The Battery, Quidi Vidi, Pouch Cove, Ferryland Head) are smaller places and can be thought about sooner. Cape Spear stays on the list as landscape, not as a likely paperwork win.

Sequence: longlist ([#11](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/11), closed) is also the shortlist ([#14](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/14), closed). Too few names to cut further on paper. The contact map ([#13](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/13), closed) is the Contact / method column in this file. Ping all of them once the pitch exists and see what gets traction. Full scoring ([#12](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/12)) stays parked. Letters ([#34](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/34)) are the send. Do not cold-contact landowners (`CONTRIBUTING.md`).

**Working lenses** (enough until #12): public access / parking, permissions path, power (grid vs solar), wind / salt, stewardship.

**Send gate:** no asks until the pitch package exists: mesh origin, test prints, Artefact quote, stage/platform, 2D sketches, Figma pitch ([#16](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/16), [#61](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/61), [#37](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/37), [#62](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/62), [#17](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/17), [#63](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/63)). The map is recorded. Sending is [#34](https://github.com/adamsimms/glowberry/issues/34). Nothing is sent.

## Exclusions (locked 2026-08-26)

- **Signal Hill:** dropped.
- **Near Pinchard's Island / Bonavista North** (Newtown, Cape Freels, Greenspond, New-Wes-Valley, the island itself): too close to resettlement. Grant language can still name Pinchard's as the relation spoken from. The object does not sit there.
- Do not treat Cartwright or Labrador harvest territory as flavor.

## 1. Fogo Island (region)

**Why.** People already travel to the island to see. That is the pilgrimage job. Shorefast and Fogo Island Arts have held landscape objects before (including Liam Gillick's *A Variability Quantifier*, the Fogo Island Red Weather Station). Category precedent, not an invitation.

**How a parcel gets named.** Introduction toward Shorefast / Zita Cobb. Ask where a ~6 ft touchable fruit could live, and who would have to say yes (town, landowner, Shorefast land, FIA). Do not pick Joe Batt's Arm vs Tilting from a map first.

**Risks.** Ferry and weather. Stewardship. Treating Shorefast as a default host. Fogo Island Arts' NL-only residency stream is closed to a Montreal-based artist (residency fact, not a siting fact).

**Power.** Rural. Hidden solar is the honest default unless a named building offers shore power.

---

## 2. Bonavista Peninsula scatter (region)

**Why.** The [Bonavista Biennale](https://bonavistabiennale.com/) already uses unconventional outdoor sites across the peninsula. A first public outing can be a 2027 festival site. A longer life needs a town or landowner after (or beside) that month. Do not collapse those two asks.

**How a parcel gets named.** Start with the existing Biennale contact. Possible scatter includes Elliston, King's Cove, Trinity, Port Rexton, Port Union, Bonavista town, and other outports the Biennale already uses. The partner names the patch. Union House Arts (Port Union) is nearby residency infrastructure, closed until a winter 2027 call. It is not a default site.

**Risks.** Biennale is presentation, not production funding. Winter emptiness. Festival month vs year-round stewardship.

**Power.** Rural / small-town. Solar default unless a specific building is in play.

---

## 3. The Battery (St. John's)

**Why.** Hill, harbour, walk-up, night access without a park gate. Neighbourhood scale suits ~6 ft better than a national historic skyline. Adjacent to Quidi Vidi, not a substitute for it.

**Risks.** Houses, parking, light complaints. The North Head Trail and Signal Hill NHS sit next door. Stay off the dropped Parks site.

**Power.** Grid more plausible than Fogo or a rural headland.

---

## 4. Quidi Vidi Village

**Why.** A village people already make a trip to. Night access as city streets. Grid and stewardship might be easier than Parks or a ferry island. Distinct from The Battery: harbour village and the Gut, not the hill.

**Risks.** Does not match the locked plateau / open-coast image as well as a headland. Tight roads, parking, residents, heritage streetscape. Mixed tenure (city plus private). A brewery-district object can read as tourism garnish.

**Access.** In St. John's.

**Power.** Grid more plausible than Fogo or a headland.

---

## 5. Pouch Cove

**Why.** Cliffs, end of the road, existing arts community, short drive from St. John's. Municipal path instead of Parks Canada.

**Risks.** Small town, neighbours, winter access, wind/salt. No contact claimed. Town hall is the first institutional door.

**Power.** Small-town grid near the road; solar on a headland.

---

## 6. Ferryland Head

**Why.** People already walk out to the light. Town-owned lighthouse (transferred to the Town of Ferryland; picnic business leases the dwelling). Not Parks Canada. Landscape matches the brief.

**Risks.** Heritage building and a strong existing brand on the same headland (Lighthouse Picnics). Competing with that destination, or looking like garnish beside it. The walk out is part of the pilgrimage; install and power on a headland are still unsolved.

**Power.** Headland. Solar default unless the town or leaseholder later offers a connection. Do not assume they will.

---

## 7. Cape Spear (kept, not preferred)

**Why.** Easternmost-point pilgrimage. Locked landscape image.

**Risks.** Parks Canada partnership, not a standard permit. Extreme wind. Keep as the gold-standard picture, not as the next email.
