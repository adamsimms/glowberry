# Glowberry — sculpture precedents & physical stress-test

Research notes for a large **glowing bakeapple** pilgrimage sculpture (**Glowberry**).

**Locked direction (see [`BRIEF.md`](BRIEF.md) + [`POWER.md`](POWER.md)):** semi-opaque translucent orange shell + internal heartbeat LEDs; touch/walk-around (no entry); ≤60" gallery plug/battery; ~10 ft outdoor solar (grid if available).

**Related:** [`BRIEF.md`](BRIEF.md) · [`POWER.md`](POWER.md)

---

## 1. Physical stress-test of *this* idea

### What has to survive (Newfoundland / Avalon / Fogo–class site)

| Stress | Implication |
|--------|-------------|
| Wind / storm surge / salt air | Anchoring, mass, corrosion, recoverability |
| UV + freeze–thaw | Material yellowing, paint failure, cracked gelcoat |
| Low winter sun | Solar autonomy for heartbeat LED (Cloudberry already failed this test once) |
| Public access / pilgrimage | Touch, climbing, photo traffic, vandalism |
| Service | Hatch for battery/LEDs without dismantling the fruit |

### Form options under stress

| Approach | Daytime read | Night glow | Durability | Fit with “resettled soul / beacon” |
|----------|--------------|------------|------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Organic FRP / translucent composite shell** (Kusama-adjacent) | Juicy, humorous, photographic | Excellent if translucent + internal LEDs | Proven outdoor; needs thickness, UV, anchor | Strong — berry as fruit of return |
| **3D-printed PETG shell** (Artefact path) | Similar if translucent / tinted | Good at smaller scales; joints matter at 50"+ | Good with UV coat; still “plastic art” | Strong for prototype → scale |
| **Geometric metal mesh / polyhedron** (Pavilion-adjacent) | Architectural, cool, less “fruit” | Can light from within/under; glow is lattice, not flesh | Excellent structural / coastal | Weaker humor; stronger “instrument / pavilion” |
| **Polished stainless fruit** (mirror cherry genre) | Spectacular, reflective sublime | Glow fights the mirror unless lit as outline | Excellent outdoors | Humor shifts to Pop luxury; less “bakeapple flesh” |
| **Inflatable / fabric** (Choi Jeong-hwa genre) | Instant spectacle | Can glow; temporary feel | Poor as permanent NL monument | Weak for long-term beacon |

**Working conclusion:** For awe + humor + uncanny *glowing flesh*, stay on the **translucent shell + internal light** path (FRP or large-format print → mold → FRP). Use **geometry / metal** as a secondary language (base, armature, cage, “receptacle”) rather than replacing the berry with a pavilion.

## 2. Primary inspirations you named

### Yayoi Kusama — *Yellow Pumpkin* (Naoshima pier, 1994; remade 2022)

| | |
|--|--|
| **What it is** | Giant yellow pumpkin with black dots on a pier; destination object people travel to photograph |
| **Size** | ~**2 m H × 2.5 m W** (~6.5 × 8 ft) — close to your Tier B thinking |
| **Material** | **Fiber-reinforced plastic (fiberglass / FRP)** |
| **Finish** | Opaque paint (not a glow piece) |
| **Stress lesson** | Typhoon (2021) swept it to sea and broke it. Remake: **~10% thicker shell**, heavier, **stem hook** for recovery / securing |
| **Why it matters for you** | Pilgrimage fruit; island site; Pop monument; humor at landscape scale. Your berry can take the *site logic* without copying polka dots. |
| **Glow?** | No — daytime icon. Night presence is ambient / flood, not internal luminescence. |
| **Links** | [CNN on reinstall](https://www.cnn.com/travel/article/kusama-naoshima-yellow-pumpkin-returns-intl-hnk); Benesse / Naoshima coverage |

**Takeaways for Glowberry**

- FRP is the default serious outdoor “giant fruit” material.  
- Design **recovery hardware** (hook / lift points) into the stem end from day one.  
- Opaque FRP = great daytime sculpture; for heartbeat glow you need **translucent FRP / resin**, not Kusama’s opaque paint system.  
- Pier / shoreline siting is powerful — and physically merciless.

### Sou Fujimoto — *Naoshima Pavilion* (2015, Miyanoura port)

| | |
|--|--|
| **What it is** | Enterable irregular **polyhedron** on the coast; “floating island” / gateway near the ferry |
| **Size** | ~**6.3–7 m** tall |
| **Material** | ~**250 white stainless-steel mesh triangles**; 12 mm bar frames welded into units on site; mesh ~37.5 mm effective grid |
| **Glow?** | **Yes — illuminated at night** (Setouchi Triennale notes). Day: translucent lattice; night: lit cage |
| **Why it matters for you** | Accessible coastal landmark; geometric / technological sublime; people go inside |
| **Links** | [Setouchi artwork page](https://setouchi-artfest.jp/en/artworks/detail/036b3ad9-887d-4b76-88f4-b7518ee970cc); [Designboom](https://www.designboom.com/architecture/sou-fujimoto-naoshima-pavilion-kagawa-japan-04-03-2015/); [Shinkenchiku fabrication notes](https://data.shinkenchiku.online/en/projects/articles/SK_2015_07_148-0) |

**If the berry went “Pavilion-geometric / metal”**

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Coastal durability; weldable; less UV paint drama | Loses juicy bakeapple humor |
| Night lighting as architecture | Glow = edges/voids, not translucent flesh |
| Enterable = pilgrimage ritual | Reads more “instrument / shelter” than fruit of return |
| Strong photo graphic | Harder to read as *your* Cloudberry title made flesh |

**Hybrid worth stress-testing:** organic translucent berry **inside** or **atop** a light metal “calyx / cage / pier frame” — Pavilion language as *support*, Kusama language as *body*.

## 2b. Primary reference shortlist (artist-selected)

These are the **best references** for Glowberry going forward:

| Reference | Steal | Don’t copy |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| **Kusama — Yellow Pumpkin** | Pilgrimage fruit; island landmark; FRP outdoor fruit | Opaque paint; polka-dot identity |
| **Paresh Maity — bronze jackfruit** | Monumental fruit as cultural metaphor; seriousness | Bronze / no glow |
| **Anish Kapoor — Cloud Gate (Chicago Bean)** | Touchable public body; uncanny scaled form people walk around | Mirror stainless as primary skin |
| **James Turrell — Skyspaces** | Light as medium; night glow / contemplative presence | Enterable architecture as the work |
| **Leo Villareal — Buckyball** | Light *as* the work; night pilgrimage; programmed “alive” LEDs | Open geodesic LED cage; full generative software (vs simple heartbeat) |

**Material lock (artist):** semi-opaque **translucent glowing orange** — FRP / PETG / equivalent light-passing systems. Interaction: touch, sit near, walk around; **no entering**.

**Villareal vs Glowberry:** Buckyball ([Madison Square Park](https://madisonsquarepark.org/art/exhibitions/leo-villareal-buckyball/)) is ~30 ft, two nested Fullerene spheres, 180 LED tubes, pixels every 1.2", custom chance-driven software — light-primary, almost no “body.” Useful if you ever pursue a **Cloudberry light-only** chapter. Glowberry keeps a **fruit body** that glows from within.

## 4. Material matrix (for *your* beacon)

| Material | Day | Night glow | Outdoor NL | Fabrication path | Comment |
|----------|-----|------------|------------|------------------|---------|
| **Translucent FRP / polyester or epoxy glass** | Excellent if tinted | **Best** for soft flesh glow | Excellent with UV gelcoat + thickness | Mold from your digital master (CNC foam / 3D print master → mold) | Closest to Kusama *structure* + Turrell/Oldenburg *light* |
| **Opaque FRP + paint** | Excellent (Kusama) | Poor unless external floods | Excellent | Same molds | Kills heartbeat concept |
| **Large-format PETG print** (Artefact) | Good | Good if translucent / thin wall | Needs UV coat; joints | Direct from STL | Ideal **prototype**; may become mold master for FRP |
| **Stainless mesh polyhedron** | Excellent graphic | Lit cage | Excellent | Welded fabrication | Pavilion path; different artwork |
| **Polished stainless solid/skin** | Mirror sublime | Hard to glow as volume | Excellent | Metal shop | Loses bakeapple juiciness |
| **Bronze / corten** | Monumental | No volume glow | Excellent | Foundry | Wrong for breathing light |
| **Inflatable PVC / nylon** | Spectacle | Possible | Poor permanent | Soft goods | Avoid for permanent beacon |

**Practical production sequence that stress-tests well**

1. Digital master (you have this)  
2. **Lit solar prototype 20–30"** in translucent PETG (Artefact / shop)  
3. If public permanent: **FRP production shell** from molds, thicker coastal spec, lift points, solar vault in base  
4. Keep heartbeat firmware dumb and robust (sine fade, low-power mode)

## 5. Geometric metal berry — verdict

A **Naoshima Pavilion–like geometric metal bakeapple** could be striking (coastal, enterable, lit at night) but it **shifts the piece** from:

- humorous / uncanny *fruit of return*  

toward:

- architectural *device / pavilion*.

That can still be “you” (resettled instrument, beacon, Cloudberry 2.0 hardware) but it spends the humor capital that makes the Photoshop composite work.

**Recommendation:** keep the **organic glowing body**; borrow Pavilion ideas for **site furniture** — pier platform, mesh wind screen, geometric plinth, or a light stainless “calyx” ring — not for the primary fruit form.

## 6. Open questions this research raises

1. **Opaque pilgrimage icon (Kusama)** vs **translucent breathing body (Oldenburg Paint Torch + Turrell night logic)** — glow locks you into translucent.  
2. Do you want people to **touch / sit near / walk around only**, or **enter** (Pavilion)? Entering a berry is funny; entering a mesh pavilion is expected.  
3. Is the first public site a **pier** (Kusama risk/reward) or **hill / trailhead** (your composite — safer wind/wave profile)?  
4. Should solar be **visible** (panels as honest Cloudberry kinship) or **hidden** (uncanny pure fruit)?
