1. What it is

Glowberry is a monumental ripe bakeapple (cloudberry) — a pilgrimage sculpture with soft heartbeat / breathing glow in the flesh at dusk and night.

Tone Awe + humor + uncanny presence
Interaction Touch, sit near, walk around, photograph — no entering
Audience Newfoundlanders and visitors
Site (outdoor) TBD — reachable NL (Avalon / Fogo / Bonavista-class); coastal plateau / shore–cliff character
Lineage Continues Cloudberry, Light House, and related beacon / resettlement work

Not: weather station, data dashboard, or enterable pavilion.

References in spirit (not to copy): Kusama Yellow Pumpkin (pilgrimage fruit); Turrell / Villareal (night light); Kapoor Cloud Gate (public touchable body) — executed as translucent glowing flesh, not opaque paint or mirror steel.

2. Look & build intent

Target: semi-opaque translucent glowing orange (amber → vermillion).

  • Hollow shell + internal diffusion + LEDs
  • Service hatch at stem / bottom
  • Daytime: readable as fruit; night: the show
  • Outdoor: occasional dark nights OK

Materials of interest

Prefer discussing Avoid as primary skin
Translucent PETG (your large-format print) Fully opaque paint
Thin translucent tinted coat + UV-stable aliphatic clear Polished stainless as the body
Or translucent FRP handoff if print isn’t right for outdoor NL

Systems (may be separate scope from shell quote)
Gallery (≤ ~60”): AC and/or battery, ~5–8 h evenings. Outdoor (~7–10 ft): solar hidden in/under base and/or grid; ESP32-class heartbeat controller — not Raspberry Pi for v1.

3. Geometry & files

Form Drupelets only (no calyx, stem, leaves, hairs)
Master ~447 × 460 × 500 mm tall; units meters; manifold / watertight
Orientation −Z = stem / attachment (bottom when upright)
Scale Uniform scale from 500 mm master per tier below
Colour STL uncolored — translucency / LEDs are finish + systems

Available on request (WeTransfer): production STL (~484k tris), preview STL, OBJ, Blender source. Preview stills and berry photo refs accompany this PDF.
